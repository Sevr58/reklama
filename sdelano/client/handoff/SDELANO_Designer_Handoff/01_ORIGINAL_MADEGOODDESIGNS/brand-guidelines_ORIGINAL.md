## Files

- `svg/` vector logo (text converted to outlines) in full colour, on dark, black and white
- `png/` transparent PNGs at 512, 1024, 2048 and 4096 px wide
- `favicon/` favicon.ico, 16–512 px icons, apple-touch-icon, site.webmanifest snippet
- `social/` profile pictures (square 400–1080 px) and header/cover images

## Usage

- Keep clear space around the logo of at least the height of the first letter.
- Use the white version on photos and dark backgrounds, the black version for one-colour print.
- Don't stretch, recolour outside this palette, add effects or rearrange the parts.
